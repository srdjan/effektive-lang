## Root-cause notes
1. Linear-constraint arithmetic has no home. `predicates.ts` (the solver), `predicate_source.ts` (compile from source) and `check_refinements.ts` (substitution at use sites) each hold a part of "linear expression -> difference constraint -> normal form". The same logic (Linear type, merge/add, difference shape test, operator table, and/or simplification) appears two or three times. Fix: one `linear.ts` next to `predicates.ts` that exports `Linear`, `addLinear`, `toDifference`, `compareLinear`, and one canonical `simplify`. Then `predicate_source.ts` and `check_refinements.ts` use it and the term-key format is defined once.
2. The program driver exists twice. `checkAnalysis` (check_project.ts) and `Program.check` (db.ts) loop over modules and call the same step, and db then patches `CheckedModule` by hand. Fix: make `checkAnalysis` take a per-module `step` hook (memo lookup) or move the loop and the interface-rebuild into check_project.ts as one function with an optional cache. Then `db.ts` holds only the memo keys and revisions, and the `buildInterface`/`hash` rule exists once.
3. `check_project.ts` is a hub because policy lives in the driver. Move the service-bound policy (`allowedServiceIds`, `serviceBoundDiagnostics`) with `requirement_provenance.ts` and `contract_origins.ts` into one `service_bounds.ts`, and the `execution-mode/003|004` and `module/004` reporting next to the pieces they report on (modes.ts, initialization.ts). The driver would then hold the scheduler and the loop only. Move `buildInitializationGraph` and its edge construction to take `references` once.
4. `Result`, `Span` live in `ir.ts`, the volatile data file, and cause the `predicates.ts`<->`ir.ts` type cycle plus a wide fan-in. Fix: a small `result.ts` (and `span.ts`, or keep them in `source.ts`) that every leaf imports. `ir.ts` then imports from `predicates.ts` in one direction, and `predicates.ts` imports no IR.
5. A new call-like builtin touches ir.ts, tables.ts, check_builtins, lower.ts, emit/shape.ts, validate/expr_walk.ts (co-change 0.77 to 1.00 with lower.ts). The compile-time `never` checks cover part. Fix inside the slice: derive `SpecialKind` and the positional-kind split from `NodeFact` in `tables.ts` once (one table of "kind -> arg shape") so that lower.ts does not keep its own list.
6. Entry-point sprawl: `frontend.ts` is a shim, `mod.ts` re-exports shims, `typescript.ts` mixes real logic and re-exports, and tests use all three. Fix: keep `frontend.ts` and `typescript.ts` as the two documented entries, make adapters and tests use only those, and keep `mod.ts` out of use (or delete it and point `deno.json` at one of them). Move `hoverAt` out of the emit-facing file into the editor side so `typescript.ts` is emit only.
