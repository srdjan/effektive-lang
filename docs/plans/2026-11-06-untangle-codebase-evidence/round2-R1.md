## Root-cause notes

Most remaining items are the same cause as the first round: a decoder or a lookup was moved to one owner for the cases that the first survey listed, but a sibling form was left out because it had another name. The record field decoder (B.H10 sibling) and the alias-or-record body lookup (B.H1 sibling) are the two clear cases. One more pass with `git grep` for the shape of each owner function would find the rest.

`optionElement` (B.H15) is the only remaining copy between the checker and the input inference engine that is a pure type query. It belongs on `TypeTable` next to `recordBodyOf`, with the other nominal queries.

The new owner modules are sound: `effect_set`, `linear`, `recursion_guard`, `referenced_ids`, `content_id`, `body_env`, `project_config`, and `service_bounds` each have a clear job, no cycle, and a caller outside their file (except `service_bounds`, which has one caller by design). I found no new hidden link in them beyond the items above.

Worth a fix, in order of leverage: (1) `recordFieldParts` in `syntax_queries.ts` (six files, and removes a name clash); (2) `TypeTable.optionElementOf` (two files); (3) `TypeTable.graphBodyOf` plus `isErrorOrTagged` at `types.ts:1222` (four files); (4) one builtin-instance id parser in `ids.ts` (three files); (5) drop `export` from five unused values; (6) `hasModifier(..., "imports")` at `declaration_index.ts:187`.
